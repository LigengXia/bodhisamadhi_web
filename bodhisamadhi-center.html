Our Lineage我们的传承

How We Got Here道场的缘起གཞུང་ལུགས་དང་བརྒྱུད་པ།

Bodhisamadhi Center was founded in 2016 in Toronto by the Buddhist master Venerable Geshe Sonam Topgyal, a graduate of Sera Mey Monastery in India — one of the three great seats of Gelug learning.

His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama has long taught that Buddhist philosophy — the Four Noble Truths, the twelve links of dependent origination, the principles of interdependence, selflessness, and the Middle Way, and the equality of all beings — preaches harmony, benevolence, and peace, and that masters should bring what they have studied for so many years into the service of a peaceful world.

World peace, he reminds us, begins in the heart. Where there is selfishness and a failure to understand interdependence, the world meets crisis after crisis. Keeping this advice close, Geshe Sonam Topgyal established Bodhisamadhi Center upon meditation and the purification of mind, as a humble contribution to peace in the world.

菩提三摩地中心由佛学导师 索南格西（Venerable Geshe Sonam Topgyal） 于 2016 年在多伦多 创立。格西毕业于印度色拉昧寺——格鲁派三大学府之一。

尊者第十四世达赖喇嘛一向教导：佛法所阐扬的四圣谛、十二缘起、缘起相依、无我、中观正见以及众生平等，宣说的正是和谐、慈悲与和平；佛门导师应当将多年所学回馈于一个和平的世界。

尊者提醒我们，世界和平始于心。人因自私、不明缘起之理，世间方危机重重。秉持此教诲，索南格西以禅修与净心为根本创建本中心，愿为世界和平略尽绵薄。

Our Vision我们的愿景

For All Sentient Beings为一切有情

Bodhisamadhi Center is committed to six aspirations rooted in compassion and the principle of interdependence:本中心以慈悲与缘起之理为本，秉持六大愿行：

📜

Ethics伦理

To uphold ethical values by drawing on the finest features of ancient and modern Tibetan and world culture.融汇古今西藏与世界文化之精华，提升伦理价值。

🧘

Meditation禅修

To ease people's distress through the practice of meditation and stillness of mind.以禅修与止观减轻众生的烦恼苦痛。

🕊️

Peace和平

To promote peace through the practice of interdependence and non-violence.以缘起相依与非暴力之行促进和平。

🙏

Prayer祈愿

To conduct prayers for the peace of the world and of Canada.为世界与加拿大的和平举行祈愿法会。

🤝

Dialogue对话

To invite masters of different faiths to speak on world peace and a harmonious society.广邀各宗教导师，畅谈世界和平与和谐社会。

📚

Translation译著

To publish and translate Buddhist books that society needs.出版与翻译社会所需之佛学典籍。

Teachings & Activities法务与共修ཆོས་ཀྱི་བྱ་བ།

Weekly每周

Yamantaka Puja

Weekly practice of the Yamantaka (Vajrabhairava) sadhana, guided by Geshe-la.每周共修大威德金刚仪轨，由格西亲自带领指点。

Bi-weekly双周

Vajrayogini Puja

Bi-weekly practice of the Vajrayogini sadhana with confession and merit accumulation.双周修持瑜伽母仪轨，循荟供忏悔、积资净障。

Monthly每月

Vajrayāna Teaching

Monthly Buddhist teaching on the Vajrayāna path — sutra and tantra explained clearly.每月金刚乘佛学开示，显密教法，观机点化。

Yearly每年

Empowerment & Fire Puja

Annual empowerment and fire puja (homa) for the traditional Gelug deities.每年随缘举行灌顶与火供，依传统格鲁本尊修法。

By request随缘

Puja by Request

Pure Land, Tara, Medicine Buddha, Dzambhala and other pujas on request.随缘修持净土、度母、药师佛、财神等各类法会。

Ongoing常态

Guidance & Phowa

Spiritual consultancy, meditation & retreat instruction, and careful phowa arrangements for the deceased.心灵咨询、禅修与闭关指导，并为亡者周密安排破瓦助念。
